FAQ

Questions & Answers

Will Undernote change the scent of my perfume?
No — Undernote is completely unscented. It creates a lightweight base layer that helps your perfume bond with your skin, so the scent you love stays true from morning to evening.
Does it work with all types of fragrance?
Undernote is designed to work well with most eau de parfum, eau de toilette, and eau de cologne formulas — whether oil-based or alcohol-based.
How do I use it?
Spray 2–3 times on skin — wrists, neck, or inner elbows. Wait 10–15 seconds for it to absorb. Then apply your fragrance on top as you normally would. Undernote is designed to help it last longer throughout the day.
